## Count the outcome correctly

A paying account means a distinct commercial customer with a settled net payment for a scoped service; record the paid period and renewal separately.
Track corporate parent, tenant, facilities and channel source separately to avoid counting duplicated accounts or expansions as new buyers.
An invited supplier, tool visitor, signup, API key, free pilot, LOI or synthetic event is not a paying account.
Report acquired and currently retained paying accounts separately; ten historical purchases do not establish ten recurring accounts.
The existing [roadmap](../../product/roadmap-2026-q2q4.md), lines 79–139, proposes outreach, SEO, referrals and developer marketing; it contains no measured acquisition mechanism. **L1 source interpretation.**
Its “20 messages to get 2–3 yeses” is a target, not an observed conversion rate; free/deeply discounted pilots are not full-price demand evidence.

## Distribution economics and phase transitions

**L2 accounting identities; every measured input is currently UNKNOWN:**
`cash_CAC = attributable sales/channel cash outlay / new independently paying accounts`; if the denominator is zero, CAC is undefined, not zero.
Report founder hours beside cash CAC, or value them explicitly in a separate economic CAC; include unsuccessful prospects and avoid counting the same labor twice.
Separate onboarding labor from acquisition; include referral commissions, partner enablement, refunds and collection delay in the cohort ledger.
`new_account_cash_need = acquisition_cash + onboarding_cash + service_cash_before_collection`; funding must precede expenditure.
Payback occurs when cumulative collected contribution after delivery costs covers acquisition plus onboarding; do not infer lifetime value without retention evidence.
Capacity must cover both new-account onboarding and existing-account support/remediation; adding sales can worsen service and cash survival.
The [economics model](economics.md), lines 86–122, demonstrates conditional staffing/cash discontinuities, not attainable distribution or actual payback. **L3 only.**
For example, its hypothetical eight onboarding hours per account would imply 80,000 hours for 10,000 accounts before support; this is arithmetic exposing a constraint, not a staffing forecast. **L2.**
No paid cohort may be labeled viable while its price, accepted workload, provider/customer effort, collection status or incident allowance is missing. **L1 decision rule.**

## Exact branch stops and best next experiment

These are proposed resource-allocation rules, not statistical significance thresholds or newly approved commercial terms.
**D1 / first 10:** run the existing market/ECON-E1 workflow as one shared experiment, not duplicate interviews; stop at 14 elapsed days or four total founder hours, whichever occurs first, with at most 90 minutes for ECON-E1's instrumented workflow. Stop that workflow at its subcap. Waiting consumes days, not attention hours.
Before any charge or real-data import, satisfy the pricing reconciliation and security/persistence gates in [economics.md](economics.md) and [security.md](security.md); no public offer is changed here.
Within D1, seek five completed economic-buyer conversations through at most ten individually authorized contacts; compare the current method against one scoped record-to-accepted-export job.
Log channel/source, delivered/contacted status, qualifications, present alternative, task/urgency, procurement owner, source consent, buyer/provider minutes, agreed terms, settled payment and subsequent submissions.
Continue that segment only if at least one buyer supplies representative consented records, accepts the scoped output and pays, with a complete ledger supporting positive contribution under explicit costs/incident allowance; otherwise stop spending on the current branch and preserve the reason.
If fewer than five conversations occur, the branch result is recruitment-limited; do not infer zero market demand. Missing commercial terms or safety gates means blocked execution, not a failed buyer hypothesis.
One paid result authorizes another bounded test only; before the 100-account branch require two independent paying accounts, each with a second unprompted operational submission and measured delivery effort. These are minimum gates, not sufficient evidence of scale.
**D2 / first 100:** only after D1 replication, cap a two-partner test at 30 days and four founder hours per partner; stop each partner branch if it yields no consented introduction to a qualified economic buyer. Do not substitute webinar attendance.
**D3 / first 1,000:** stop connector/distribution implementation before it starts unless three independent paying firms need the same mapping and the channel owner explicitly grants distribution access; pause after any measured cohort has negative contribution or unmet delivery capacity.
**D4 / first 10,000:** defer entirely until prior-scale paid retention, reachable-account counts, channel access and funded delivery capacity are evidenced; another spreadsheet forecast does not clear the gate.
**All stages:** stop acquisition increases on quote/charge mismatch, unauthorized data sharing, tenant-isolation failure, or unfunded promised service; continue only after the specific failure is corrected and independently rechecked.
