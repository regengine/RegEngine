## Competing hypotheses

| Hypothesis (all L0–L1) | Decisive disconfirmation |
| --- | --- |
| V0: ERP-adjacent evidence subscription for processors. | Existing ERP/template/provider already completes the task at acceptable cost. |
| V1: Preflight and source reconciliation alongside existing traceability networks. | Incumbent already fixes the source gaps, buyer will not pay a second vendor, or downstream recipient rejects the output. |
| V2: Bounded assisted readiness/recall deliverable before recurring self-service. | Custom cleanup consumes more than the price or the buyer does not repeat the job. |

V2 is an experiment, not authorization to launch a consulting business. No claim
that any hypothesis has an uncontested market survived this review.

## Smallest external test

First reconcile one explicit offer internally. Then the founder can choose a
warm-contact workflow: five short conversations about recent records and current
alternatives; request consented, minimized sample data from qualified buyers;
observe one lot through both their present method and the bounded RegEngine job.
The existing $499 Base display is a possible test anchor, not a chosen new price
and not actual willingness to pay.

Proposed budget cap: two founder hours discovery and two delivery hours, excluding
recruitment waiting time. No ads, honoraria, or new infrastructure. Record source
coverage, correction time, founder minutes, repeat submission, recipient acceptance,
and actual payment. Two buyers identifying a concrete unmet task and supplying
usable records plus one payment justify further testing; payment establishes L7
only for that buyer/offer. Nonresponse tests the channel, not zero market demand.
These thresholds are experimental decision rules, not statistical proof.

Result: NOT RUN. Strongest reason to pause feature expansion: no located customer
proof plus established competing offers. Verdict: MODIFY the next validation
cycle toward actual records and paid outcomes. Demand confidence did not increase
from this internal research.
