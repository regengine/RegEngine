# IFPA + Western Growers cold-email draft

Last updated: 2026-05-26

Single high-leverage send to the two largest fresh-produce trade groups.
Body asks each org's member-services team to help reach 20 of their
members for 30-minute FSMA 204 discovery conversations. One send to
each org *potentially* reaches hundreds of operators through their
channels — vs. one-at-a-time LinkedIn outreach.

## Verify these addresses before sending

I'm using standard membership-services email patterns. Confirm against
each org's contact page before clicking send:

- **IFPA** (International Fresh Produce Association)
  - Likely: `info@freshproduce.com` or `membership@freshproduce.com`
  - Source of truth: https://www.freshproduce.com/contact/
- **Western Growers Association**
  - Likely: `membership@wga.com` or `info@wga.com`
  - Source of truth: https://www.wga.com/contact

## Email body (paste into your client)

**To:** `[verified IFPA / WGA membership email]`
**Subject:** FSMA 204 discovery — would your member-services team help reach 20 of your members?

```
Hi —

I'm a solo founder building a tool that helps fresh-produce processors
and First Land-Based Receivers actually meet FSMA 204's 24-hour FDA
spreadsheet requirement when it goes into effect July 2028.

I'm in discovery and would value help reaching ~20 of your member
companies for 30-minute conversations on what their teams are doing
today for CTE / KDE capture, what FoodLogiQ / Trace Register /
ReposiTrak quotes have looked like, and where current systems fall
short.

Two ways I could imagine this working:

  1. You share my Calendly link in a member newsletter or alert —
     https://calendly.com/regengine/meet-and-greet — I do all the
     scheduling myself.
  2. You introduce me to 5–10 members directly; I follow up one-to-one.

Happy to share back what I learn from these 20 calls if it's useful
for your members. No sponsorship pitch, no sales motion — I'm just
trying to build the right thing for a deadline that's now 25 months
out.

— Christopher Sellers
   RegEngine — https://regengine.co
```

## Why this works

- **One send, hundreds of impressions.** Trade orgs are the right
  amplifier; they already have permission to email members.
- **Low risk on their side.** You're not asking them to endorse
  anything, just forward a Calendly link or make 5–10 intros.
- **Reciprocity, not money.** Offering to share back what you learn
  is the lightweight value exchange that fits a founder's budget AND
  member-services' incentive (members get something out of it).

## Follow-up plan

If no response in 7 days, send a one-line follow-up: *"Bumping this in
case it got buried — happy to keep it simple. Open to any path that
gets me in front of 10–20 of your FSMA-204-affected members."*

If a yes comes back: respond same-day with a one-page brief of what the
discovery calls will cover, so member-services can forward it confidently.
