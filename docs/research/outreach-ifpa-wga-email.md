# IFPA + Western Growers cold-email draft

Last updated: 2026-05-23

Single high-leverage send to the two largest fresh-produce trade groups.
Body asks each org's member-services team to broker $100 paid discovery
calls with their FSMA-204-affected members. One send to each org
*potentially* reaches hundreds of operators through their channels —
vs. one-at-a-time LinkedIn outreach.

## Verify these addresses before sending

I'm using standard membership-services email patterns. Confirm against
each org's contact page before clicking send:

- **IFPA** (International Fresh Produce Association)
  - Likely: `info@freshproduce.com` or `membership@freshproduce.com`
  - Source of truth: https://www.freshproduce.com/contact/
- **Western Growers Association**
  - Likely: `membership@wga.com` or `info@wga.com`
  - Source of truth: https://www.wga.com/contact

## Email body (paste into your client)

**To:** `[verified IFPA / WGA membership email]`
**Subject:** $100 / 30 minutes — FSMA 204 readiness research with your members

```
Hi —

I'm a solo founder building a tool that helps fresh-produce processors and
First Land-Based Receivers actually meet FSMA 204's 24-hour FDA spreadsheet
requirement when it goes into effect July 2028.

I'm in paid discovery — paying $100 each for 30 minutes with QA managers,
food-safety directors, and compliance leads at your member companies.
Honestly, I'd rather hear from 20 of them than guess. I'm trying to
understand:

  - What they're doing today for CTE / KDE capture
  - What FoodLogiQ, Trace Register, and ReposiTrak quotes have looked like
  - Where their current systems fall short of the rule

Would your member-services team be open to coordinating? Two ways I could
imagine this working:

  1. You share my Calendly link in a member newsletter or alert —
     https://calendly.com/regengine/meet-and-greet — I do all the
     scheduling and payment myself.
  2. You introduce me to 5–10 members directly; I follow up one-to-one.

Either path, I cover the $100 per call out of my own budget — this isn't
a sponsorship pitch and I'm not selling anything yet.

Happy to share what I learn back to your team after 20 calls if it's
useful for your members.

— Christopher Sellers
   RegEngine — https://regengine.co
```

## Why this works

- **One send, hundreds of impressions.** Trade orgs are the right
  amplifier; they already have permission to email members.
- **$100 is real money on the table.** Member-services teams routinely
  field "would you forward this to your members" asks; most have nothing
  in it for the org. Yours has paid research that members will value.
- **Low risk on their side.** You're not asking them to endorse anything,
  just forward a Calendly link.

## Follow-up plan

If no response in 7 days, send a one-line follow-up: *"Bumping this in
case it got buried — happy to keep it simple. Open to any path that gets
me in front of 10–20 of your FSMA-204-affected members."*

If a yes comes back: respond same-day with a one-page brief of what the
discovery calls will cover, so member-services can forward it confidently.
