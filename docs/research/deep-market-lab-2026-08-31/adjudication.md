# Independent adjudication

**Date:** 2026-08-31. **Role:** planner, independent judgment. **Decision:** **MODIFY, with a strict learning budget.** Preserve the ERP-adjacent reconciliation idea as an option, pause a broad launch, and compare a bounded assisted service with a recurring product. This is a recommendation, not authorization to contact prospects, collect private records, change prices, or deploy.

## Position A — continue a narrow product experiment

The strongest argument is the alignment between an observable task and existing implementation: diagnose missing supplier fields, correct records, persist them, and return an understandable artifact. Public FDA tabletop evidence in the [regulatory memo](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/regulation.md) identifies coordination and completeness problems, although its volunteer sample cannot establish industry prevalence. The current Inflow interface supports a practical supervised demonstration without another redesign. [Product assessment](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/product.md)

The repository also contains useful fail-closed controls, typed records, tenant context, and working tests. These reduce the cost of testing a bounded workflow. They do not prove deployed reliability, but they make wholesale abandonment premature if an accessible operator has a recurring unmet task. The right unit of value is the completed correction or request response, not a dashboard view or a cryptographic badge.

**What would support A:** an operator supplies an authorized representative file, shows the current process, completes a second real task with reusable mappings and less total labor, and a buyer accepts a concrete recurring offer. Every condition remains unverified.

## Position B — freeze product expansion and test a service first

This position is stronger than merely “be cautious.” Competitors already advertise CSV intake, validation, exports, and assisted readiness; iFoodDS has a public entry offer below RegEngine's displayed partner anchors. A new evidence layer can create another reconciliation obligation. Existing software or a small internal report may already solve the buyer's actual problem. [Market comparison](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/market.md)

Security and engineering findings concern the proposed value itself. Chain verification checks stored hashes without rehashing current event content; historical export reproduction disagrees with producer modes/types; changed quantities can share an idempotency key. CSV calls compatibility ingestion, so selecting CSV does not automatically inherit the stronger webhook V2 transaction properties. [Security findings](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/security.md), [engineering findings](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/engineering.md)

A bounded rehearsal could discover whether customers buy the founder's help, repeatable software, or neither. It must disclose limits and cannot be sold as certification. If only one-time work sells, that is a services result; recurring SaaS conversion must not be assumed. An API is attractive only after an integrator brings repeated matching work. A channel is evidenced by qualified introductions and successful delivery, not a partnership agreement or new reseller portal.

**What would support B:** useful paid outcomes remain bespoke, the customer needs occasional expert review rather than monitoring, or incumbent tools handle the automated portion adequately. Stop product expansion while evaluating whether service delivery is economical and within the provider's expertise.

## Evidence audit and dissent

The strongest evidence is narrow: reproduced content-verification and savepoint behavior, inspected pricing contradictions, and official documentation of competitors and regulation. Passing mocked tests establish those tested behaviors, not whole-system safety. Findings from overlapping test selections should not be presented as hundreds of independent end-to-end validations. Scaling formulas identify useful measurements, but they are not measured capacity. [Scaling limits](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/scaling.md)

The weakest evidence concerns purchasing and retention. Five recorded sends with blank outcomes do not establish either demand or rejection. Multiple agents agreeing on an ICP add no independent customer observations.

The [economics inputs](../../../docs/research/deep-market-lab-2026-08-31/economics_inputs.json) invent demand, churn, support, incident, and capital assumptions. Monte Carlo repeats these assumptions; its loss fractions and survival figures are not business odds. Higher modeled prices mechanically improve margins while demand is held unchanged. The defensible lesson is to measure labor and stop promising uncapped free support, not to select the price with the most favorable simulated distribution.

**Dissent from the provisional recommendation:** “do not kill without demand evidence” cannot justify indefinite work. A founder may rationally pause after a capped test produces no accessible customer or usable file, even though the market has not been disproved. Conversely, correcting every lane's findings before talking to an operator would turn this audit into another internal project. Current-process interviews need no new product release. Hosted private-data use and trusted evidence claims do require the relevant safety gates.

## Unknowns that could reverse the decision

- **Acceptance:** who must receive the output, in what channel, and will they accept an additional system's artifact?
- **Control:** can the buyer obtain missing supplier facts, authorize processing, and approve corrective records? Software cannot compensate for absent cooperation.
- **Counterfactual:** does RegEngine save total operator plus founder effort against the existing process, rather than shifting work to the founder?
- **Cadence:** is the next valuable job weekly, seasonal, or an occasional drill? Forced weekly activity can mistake instrumented usage for demand; measure the next natural task.
- **Trust cost:** will a customer permit sensitive supply-chain data in a pre-production system without references, a credible continuity plan, and qualified domain review?

These are unresolved questions, not newly asserted market facts.

## Ranked first-week actions and resolving experiments

1. **Define the boundary and cap.** Choose one operation, one file type, one recipient, and a fixed founder-hour budget. Draft one consistent offer; do not route a willingness-to-pay test through the current conflicting checkout contract.
2. **Observe the current process first.** After explicit outreach authorization, obtain one operator walkthrough and permissioned redacted file. Record a real failure, total staff time, source ownership, and budget owner before showing RegEngine. If no suitable participant is available, improve recruitment once; do not build another workflow.
3. **Make a truth checklist.** With qualified traceability review, map the selected receiving/shipping task to required fields and source rows. Compare a manual/service result with RegEngine. Include missing facts and all human preparation time. This tests usefulness even before a trusted production pilot.
4. **Gate only the selected evidence path.** Before relying on customer evidence, verify tenant isolation in the intended environment, changed-payload retry behavior, persisted record reconciliation, export row-set completeness, and truthful verification semantics. Keep optional graph sync and unselected intake paths outside this pilot. Synthetic demonstration remains explicitly separate.
5. **Make a stop/continue decision.** Require a concrete buyer next step and a plausible second natural task before expanding. If service sells but recurrence does not, evaluate service economics. If neither produces commitment within the agreed cap, pause this wedge. Do not substitute more simulations for those observations.

**Confidence:** high that broad launch and compliance claims exceed current evidence; medium that a bounded assisted experiment is worth trying; low that the proposed segment supports a profitable recurring product. No numerical probability is justified.
