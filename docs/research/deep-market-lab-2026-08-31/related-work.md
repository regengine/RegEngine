This run corroborated that historical schema mismatch by inspecting the base implementation:

- At the inspected base, the [FDA package generator](../../../services/ingestion/app/fda_export_service.py) emitted entries containing `name` and `sha256` only. The integrated generator now also emits `path`, `size`, and manifest Merkle metadata.

The historical evidence level is a local controlled reproduction reported and preserved by the related task, plus direct static corroboration here. The integrated repair now accepts declared `VERIFIED` and `PARTIAL` packages through the API and SDK, and rejects byte tampering, self-consistent `UNVERIFIED` status, manifest/hashed-chain disagreement, and undeclared or duplicate ZIP members. These remain local contract tests, not a live FDA response or production test. They do not independently recompute the persisted chain, authenticate the issuer, or prove source-data accuracy.
