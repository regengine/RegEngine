# Claim-evidence graph

Graph form: **claim → evidence → assumptions → counterevidence → independent verification → confidence → falsifier**. Source files in the lane reports are the detailed evidence nodes. Confidence always applies to the narrow claim, not to company success.

Labels: VERIFIED FACT (source/code observation), STRONG INFERENCE (supported mechanism), WEAK INFERENCE (limited extrapolation), HYPOTHESIS (proposed behavior), UNKNOWN (not observed). Reality levels: 0 idea, 1 logical, 2 mathematical, 3 simulated, 4 historical, 5 controlled experiment, 6 realistic sandbox, 7 limited real world, 8 repeated real world, 9 scaled real world. A level-5 function test does not promote the entire product to level 5.

## Evidence provenance and conflict handling

Primary web material establishes the published rule or vendor offer, not vendor efficacy or RegEngine demand. Historic/composite quotes in the May GTM document remain hypotheses even if several agents agreed. New test results are attributed by lane; overlapping tests are not summed into a unique total. Original related-work counts remain separately reported even though the reviewed changes were later integrated.

The assumption ledger uses impact × uncertainty to prioritize **research**. A known severe defect has low uncertainty and therefore a low research score; that never reduces its priority as a release blocker. Confirmed defects move from investigation to a scoped repair with regression evidence. The [experiment backlog](experiments.md) records those separate gates.
