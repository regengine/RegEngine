# Decision-ranked experiment backlog

These are proposed scoped actions, not an instruction to send messages, spend money, change production, or override existing promises. Costs/times are planning hypotheses. Customer contact requires explicit authorization. There is no scheduled continuation. Owners below are proposed work lanes, not external assignments.

**Resource cap recommendation:** ten founder hours across fourteen days for discovery, at most two observed sessions, no paid acquisition. Run discovery in parallel with one narrowly chosen technical repair. Do not repair the whole repo before asking about a current workflow. Do not use hosted output as trusted evidence before that route's integrity and tenant gates pass. See [independent adjudication](adjudication.md).

## E01 — Does a reachable buyer have a costly present task?

**Question/hypothesis:** one covered operator can show a recent failed or labor-intensive reconciliation task, authority to share a redacted example, and a budget owner. **Information value:** highest; can invalidate the wedge. **Cost/time:** no ad spend; up to five screened conversations within the founder-hour cap. **Dependencies:** authorized contact and consent; no application fix required for a current-process interview. **Method:** inspect the actual prior task and current alternative before showing RegEngine; distinguish supplier waiting time from operator work. **Success:** at least two independent examples and one buyer willing to discuss a bounded paid outcome. **Failure:** no concrete repeated problem or incumbent/manual method already satisfactory. **Result:** NOT RUN; demand remains UNKNOWN. **Next:** E02/E10 if evidence supports it; otherwise narrow or pause the offer rather than add features. Small samples guide learning, not a market conversion estimate.

## E02 — Can the observed outcome produce acceptable economics?

**Hypothesis:** a bounded file/drill produces enough saved operator effort to pay for measured delivery. **Information value:** highest; tests business rather than code elegance. **Cost/time:** two observation sessions plus analysis inside the same ten-hour cap. **Dependencies:** E01 records, consent, one coherent nonbinding offer; customer-hosted/manual path may be used while software gates remain open. **Method:** compare usual process and assisted RegEngine path; record all rows, unresolved sources, founder/supplier/customer minutes, rework and provider usage separately. Present a fixed scope, price and support limit without charging. **Success:** buyer accepts the value/scope and observed cost fits it; pre-register an illustrative 50% reduction in operator work with no completeness loss. **Failure:** work merely shifts to founder/supplier, unresolved records increase, or accepted price cannot cover delivery. **Result:** hypothetical model completed, real economics NOT RUN. **Next:** replace model inputs; compare service-only versus recurring offer. Do not treat the 50% gate as a measured benchmark.

## E03 — Does one commercial offer survive the payment contract?

**Hypothesis:** displayed price, billing interval, partner eligibility, facilities, event limits and entitlement agree. **Information value:** high; current mismatch can invalidate any payment experiment. **Cost/time:** one scoped engineering day after founder chooses terms. **Dependencies:** one documented offer preserving existing commitments; test-mode configuration if available, never a real charge. **Method:** frontend quote → plan map → checkout price metadata → webhook → entitlement; include annual/monthly, aliases, unavailable prices, unauthorized tenant and unsupported plan cases. **Success:** one exact documented amount/period/envelope through every stage. **Failure:** silent fallback, differing interval/amount or mismatched entitlement. **Result:** STATIC CONTRACT CONTRADICTION FOUND; live charges UNKNOWN. **Next:** scoped billing change with explicit callout and negative tests; no automatic pricing decision in this report.

## E04 — Is an original package accepted and a changed package rejected?

**Hypothesis:** the actual generator and advertised verifiers implement a compatible versioned contract and accurately describe their limits. **Information value:** high, cheap local falsifier. **Cost/time:** half to one engineering day; no customer data. **Dependencies:** inspect [related work](related-work.md) first to reuse existing fixes and probes. **Method:** actual FDA package → SDK/public helper; inspect persisted and current content separately; unchanged artifact, changed member, missing/duplicate/unsafe path, malformed metadata and unsupported version cases. **Success:** supported original passes; corrupt data fails; unsupported format is distinct from tampering; no issuer-authentication claim without a trusted anchor. **Failure:** original rejected or changed content falsely approved under the declared contract. **Result:** baseline package schema mismatch reproduced. Post-integration, API/SDK checks accept declared `VERIFIED` and `PARTIAL` packages and reject byte tampering, a self-consistent `UNVERIFIED` chain, manifest/hashed-chain disagreement, and undeclared or duplicate ZIP members. Stored-content mutation, independent chain recomputation, and trusted-root/issuer-authentication limits remain. **Confidence update:** the compatibility and declared failed-chain defects are repaired locally; broad authenticity/provenance claims remain unsupported. **Next:** keep compatibility, malformed/unsafe-path, status-consistency, and tamper regressions; align assurance wording without claiming authenticity.

## E05 — Does the selected pilot intake preserve durable evidence and corrections?

**Hypothesis:** accepted input survives failure/retry exactly once with canonical/legacy/evaluation/export agreement. **Information value:** high safety gate. **Cost/time:** one engineering day estimate. **Dependencies:** disposable PostgreSQL with migrations and intended non-superuser role, synthetic tenants A/B; choose CSV compatibility or another exact route rather than assume V2 guarantees apply. **Method:** forced canonical failure, commit timeout, worker interruption, repeat unchanged record, same-identity changed quantity/UoM, malformed input, hostile tenant claims and pooled reuse; reconcile identifiers across stores. **Success:** acceptance means durable complete evidence or explicit recoverable quarantine; changed content conflicts/amends; no cross-tenant result. **Failure:** accepted evidence missing, silent correction loss or leakage. **Result:** component EPCIS/correction counterexamples found; actual PostgreSQL integration NOT RUN. **Next:** minimum transaction/repair change on the selected route. Optional graph sync stays off; nest-aware sync regression is necessary only before enabling that secondary path.

## E06 — Does the regulatory contract match one real operator?

**Hypothesis:** required KDEs/citations/coverage and exemptions match the selected activity. **Information value:** high, avoids building unnecessary remediation. **Cost/time:** half to one day of qualified review; reviewer availability/cost unknown. **Dependencies:** operator facts and current primary rule, not legacy MVP text. **Method:** shipping plus receiving examples, ordinary non-GTIN TLC, TLC source and source reference, document type/number, exempt supplier, two-input transformation and default/full-data exports. **Success:** correct applicability and every required requested field is understandable; score is separate from legal conclusion. **Failure:** required field omitted, wrong citation, or internal 80% score labeled a regulatory safe harbor. **Result:** rule/citation/copy contradictions documented; recipient acceptance NOT RUN. **Next:** bounded rule/copy corrections with fixtures before a compliance claim. This does not promise legal certification.

## E07 — Can exports remain complete and historically explainable?

**Hypothesis:** source membership, filters, privacy mode and formatter version produce verifiable retained artifacts. **Information value:** high evidence gate. **Cost/time:** half to one engineering day after E04/E05. **Dependencies:** isolated fixture/DB; no real regulator request. **Method:** 10,001 distinct lots, mixed types, expanded transformation lots, late arrivals, empty export, both PII modes, full/filtered CSV/package, two actors requesting same artifact. **Success:** every requested record accounted for; truncation survives in retained artifact metadata; original exports remain explainable after new data; both actors recoverable. **Failure:** missing lots, extra types, false corruption alarm, 409 for supported producer type or lost actor attempt. **Result:** baseline privacy-mode/type mismatches reproduced; the integrated local probe now passes both privacy modes and both full-export labels. Full-set DB, late-arrival/as-of membership, and actor-history tests remain NOT RUN. **Next:** test and persist exact historical membership and audit actors without relaxing privacy controls.

## E08 — Can evidence survive a provider failure or ended subscription?

**Hypothesis:** authorized custody/retrieval survives restart, backup restore and billing/provider outage. **Information value:** high; trust promise may outlast revenue. **Cost/time:** half-day tabletop then disposable restore, runtime/cost not yet measured. **Dependencies:** test backup and controlled intended application role; no production credential access. **Method:** retained artifact verification, restore known tenant A/B fixtures, billing-cache loss/inactive state, logger restart and original-versus-later exports. **Success:** recoverable authorized records with clear service boundaries; both tenants remain isolated. **Failure:** irreversible evidence loss, hidden dependency on active subscription, or elevated-role workaround. **Result:** NOT RUN. **Next:** a documented supported custody/retention envelope; no blanket infrastructure redesign.

## E09 — What limits appear at concentrated traffic and long history?

**Hypothesis:** supported volume can be stated from measurements rather than average-event arithmetic. **Information value:** medium before pilot, high before SLA. **Cost/time:** half-day bounded local/disposable experiment; hardware budget separately authorized if needed. **Dependencies:** correctness gates E05/E07, stated machine/DB and fixtures. **Method:** 10 through one million equivalent events/day analytically; materialize only necessary boundary sizes; compare one hot tenant and many tenants, 10× burst, backfill, full-history verification and exports. Measure SQL count, lock wait, RSS, p95 latency, error rate and recovery. **Success:** reproducible envelope with explicit refusal/backpressure beyond limits. **Failure:** silent partial evidence or resource exhaustion below offered volume. **Result:** model and code bottlenecks inspected; no load capacity measured. **Next:** optimize measured choke point only; no speculative Kubernetes or sharding.

## E10 — Is the winning result repeatable without founder dependence?

**Hypothesis:** a second operator completes the next naturally recurring task at lower support cost and provides a real referral or recurring commitment. **Information value:** highest after first outcome. **Cost/time:** one follow-up session at the task's natural cadence, not forced weekly clicking. **Dependencies:** E01/E02 evidence and any needed hosted-route gates. **Method:** new source batch/drill, another operator; compare service-only, subscription and integrator channel fairly. Track supplier authority, buyer acceptance and all assistance. **Success:** repeat value plus a concrete paid recurring scope or a repeatable paid service; only then test two consultant-referred cases. **Failure:** one-off deadline task, manual heroics, no buyer value or differing custom product for every client. **Result:** NOT RUN. **Next:** CONTINUE winning bounded offer, PIVOT to service if that alone sells, or PAUSE at resource cap. One win is not product-market fit.

## Stopping and escalation rules

Stop research branches when a scoped counterexample answers the question; convert them to repair gates instead of producing another audit. Stop speculative mass-market, consumer/gamified, broad regulatory-platform and blockchain investigations because no current evidence links them to this spine. Financial-market volatility/liquidity simulations are not relevant to a B2B traceability subscription; model customer concentration, cash timing and operational shocks instead. No patent-clearance exercise was performed, and no freedom-to-operate claim is made.

Pause the product experiment when the agreed founder-hour/cash limit is reached without new evidence, even if market rejection has not been proven. A restart requires new external evidence or a cheaper decisive experiment. Continue internal work only when it removes a concrete chosen-workflow blocker; never consume agent capacity merely to preserve momentum.
