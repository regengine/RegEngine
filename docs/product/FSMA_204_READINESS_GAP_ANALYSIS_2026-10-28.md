## 7. AI-authority boundary against the repo

The external summary says AI may suggest mappings, extract candidates, explain errors and summarize drills, and may not invent KDEs, approve exceptions, determine compliance or silently merge identities.

- Jev header mapping and file-CTE suggestions are advisory by default and recorded as `field_mapping_suggestion` artifacts (`services/shared/jev/header_mapping.py`, `services/shared/jev/cte_classification.py`; `docs/architecture/decisions/ADR-005-jev-header-mapping-suggestions.md`). Two switches, `REGENGINE_JEV_APPLY_HEADER_MAPPINGS` and `REGENGINE_JEV_APPLY_FILE_CTE_TYPE`, are read from the environment in `services/shared/jev/settings.py` (default off) and consumed in `services/ingestion/app/csv_templates.py`. When either is on, suggestions at or above the `auto_apply` threshold of 0.90 in `services/shared/jev/routing.py` are applied to the ingested rows without per-item human confirmation. The external boundary ("suggest only") holds in the default configuration and is narrower than what the switches allow; `docs/PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION.md` already describes the switches as opt-in.
- LlamaIndex extraction of PDFs stages rows in the upload session with a review warning; writing them needs a separate `POST /commit` (`services/admin/app/bulk_upload/routes.py`; `docs/architecture/decisions/ADR-006-llamaindex-extract-supplier-pdfs.md`). Consistent.
- Identity merges happen only through `merge_entities` in `services/ingestion/app/identity_router.py` or review resolution; the matcher is lexical, and matches at or above 0.90 are queued as "likely" rather than merged. Consistent with "no silent merge".
- Exception waivers: `PATCH /{case_id}/waive` in `services/ingestion/app/exception_router.py` requires `exceptions.write`, but `waiver_approved_by` comes from the request body, not the authenticated principal. This is not an AI path. It does weaken "may not approve exceptions" as a control, because the recorded approver is caller-supplied. Disposition: fold into SEL-463 (signed review audit trail, F8); story S5.
