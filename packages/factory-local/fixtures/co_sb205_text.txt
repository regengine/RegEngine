Colorado SB24-205 (Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence), C.R.S.
6-1-1701 et seq., paraphrased for this demo:

C.R.S. 6-1-1703 (deployer duties). A deployer of a high-risk artificial
intelligence system that does business in Colorado completes an impact
assessment of that system at least annually. A deployer with fewer than fifty
full-time equivalent employees is exempt from this duty when it meets the
conditions set out in the statute.

C.R.S. 6-1-1706 (enforcement). The attorney general has exclusive enforcement
authority. A violation is treated as a deceptive trade practice, with civil
penalties of up to $20,000 per violation under the Colorado Consumer
Protection Act. The statute creates no private right of action. A deployer
has an affirmative defense when it complies with a recognized risk management
framework such as the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.
